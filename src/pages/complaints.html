Complaints and redress

If something goes wrong, tell us. Here is how to raise it and what to expect.

Raising a complaint

Landlords and tenants can complain by email to {{cfg:contact.email}}. Please include the property address, what happened and what you would like us to do about it.

We will acknowledge your complaint, look into it properly and reply in writing with what we found and what we will do. If you are still unhappy, you can ask for the complaint to be escalated within GETLET.
