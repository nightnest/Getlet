The challenges

What worries landlords living abroad

  • Time zones. Waiting for approval across time zones can leave a repair waiting.
  • Invoices. You want to see what was spent, and why.
  • Distance. You cannot attend viewings, hand over keys or let a contractor in.
  • UK obligations. Landlord duties still apply when you live elsewhere.

How GETLET helps

  • Marketing on Rightmove, Zoopla and social media
  • Rent collected through one system and the balance paid on to you
  • Repairs through a pre-vetted contractor network
  • Every deduction recorded as an invoice
  • Gas and electrical certificates coordinated through partner agencies

Choosing a plan

Fully Managed is often the simplest fit from abroad

In Fully Managed, GETLET orders repairs directly within written delegated authority, so a boiler fault does not wait for you to wake up. In Limited Management you keep every decision, but your approval time affects how quickly work can start.

Either way, the limits are agreed in writing. Compare the plans.

GETLET does not give tax advice. If you live abroad, speak to a qualified accountant about how UK rental income is taxed. We can arrange onboarding and updates remotely by email or phone.
