Talk to us about switching

Before you move

Four things to check first

  1. Read your current agreement. Look for the notice period and any fee for ending it early.
  2. Gather your records. Tenancy agreement, gas and electrical certificates, EPC, deposit details, inventory and recent statements.
  3. Confirm the money. Check what rent and deposit your agent holds, and how and when it will be passed on.
  4. Tell your tenant. Your tenant needs to know who to pay and who to contact.

This is general guidance, not legal advice. Your own agreement sets the rules for your situation.
