The repair workflow

From report to rent statement

  1. Tenant reports the problem to GETLET.
  2. GETLET checks the management type. Fully Managed: we order directly. Limited Management: we ask you first.
  3. A contractor attends from the pre-vetted network.
  4. The invoice is added to PayProp against your property.
  5. The cost is deducted from the next rent and appears on your statement.
