The PayProp model

Four steps from tenant to landlord

{{icon:home}}

1. Tenant pays rent

Rent is paid into the PayProp-linked RBS account.

{{icon:receipt}}

2. Work becomes an invoice

Every piece of work done on a property is entered into PayProp as an invoice.

{{icon:refresh}}

3. Deductions happen automatically

When rent arrives, PayProp deducts contractor invoices and expenses.

{{icon:wallet}}

4. You receive the balance

PayProp sends the remaining balance to the landlord.

Sample statement

How to read a landlord rent statement

This example shows the shape of a statement: money in, each deduction, and what is paid out. It is deliberately simple.

  • Rent received is the starting point.
  • Each deduction names what it was for.
  • The landlord payout is the rent left after every deduction.
{{icon:shield}}

An audit trail by design

Because every piece of work is entered as an invoice before it is deducted, each deduction has an invoice behind it.
