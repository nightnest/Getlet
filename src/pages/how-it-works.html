How GETLET works

Nine steps from handing over your property to receiving your rent, with a record of every cost along the way.

The nine steps

  1. {{icon:clipboard}}Step 1

    Landlord submits the property

    You tell us about the property and what you are looking for, using our valuation form.

  2. {{icon:file}}Step 2

    GETLET agrees the management arrangement

    You choose between Fully Managed and Limited Management, and we set out the arrangement in writing. Tenancy agreement and deposit arrangements are covered in your written agreement.

  3. {{icon:shield}}Step 3

    Compliance certificates are coordinated through partner agencies

    Gas and electrical certificates are arranged through specialist partner agencies. The landlord's legal duty remains with the landlord.

  4. {{icon:megaphone}}Step 4

    Property is marketed across Rightmove, Zoopla and social media

    Your property is advertised on the main property portals and promoted on Facebook, Instagram and TikTok.

  5. {{icon:users}}Step 5

    A suitable tenant is found

    Enquiries are handled and viewings arranged until a suitable tenant is found. Referencing is covered in your written agreement.

  6. {{icon:key}}Step 6

    Tenant moves in and rent is collected

    Rent is paid into the PayProp-linked account from the start of the tenancy.

  7. {{icon:wrench}}Step 7

    Maintenance requests are handled

    The tenant reports a problem, GETLET checks your management type and a vetted contractor attends. In Limited Management, we ask for your approval first.

  8. {{icon:receipt}}Step 8

    Expenses are recorded in PayProp

    Every piece of work done on your property is entered as an invoice, creating a clear audit trail.

  9. {{icon:wallet}}Step 9

    The remaining rent is paid to the landlord

    When rent arrives, invoices and expenses are deducted automatically and the balance is paid to you. Payment dates are set out in your management agreement.
