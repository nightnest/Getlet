Privacy and cookies

What this website does with your information, in plain English.

Who we are

GETLET is the controller of the personal information you give us through this website. You can contact us at {{cfg:contact.email}}.

Enquiry and repair forms

We use the details you enter only to respond to your enquiry or repair report. We ask only for what we need at this stage. Optional marketing emails are separate: they need their own tick box, which is never pre-ticked, and you can unsubscribe at any time.

When you send a form, your details are passed to us through Formspree, a form-handling service that delivers them to our inbox. We use them only to respond to you. If the form cannot send, you will be told and can email us directly at the address above.

Cookies and storage

  • Essential: this site stores your cookie choice in your browser so it does not ask on every page.
  • Analytics (optional): if you accept analytics, anonymised usage events help us see which pages are useful. If you reject, nothing is loaded. These events never include your name, email address, phone number, address or message text.
  • Campaign source: if you arrive from a tagged link, its source, medium and campaign are kept for your browser session so an enquiry can be traced back to where you came from.
