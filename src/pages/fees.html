Before you sign

What to ask any agent before you sign

These questions apply to GETLET and to every other agent you speak to. Ask for the answers in writing.

  • What is the total cost over the first 12 months, including VAT?
  • Is the management charge based on rent due or rent collected?
  • Are there setup, inspection, renewal or rent review fees?
  • Who pays for gas, electrical and EPC work, and who arranges it?
  • Are repair invoices passed on at cost, or is there an arrangement fee?
  • How are major works authorised, and is there a spending limit?
  • What happens to my money if the agent stops trading? Which client money protection scheme are they in?
  • Which redress scheme can I use if something goes wrong?
  • How long is the contract, and what does it cost to leave?
  • Who is my named contact, and how will I be updated?
