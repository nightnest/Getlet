Fully Managed or Limited Management

Two ways to let your property. The difference is who makes the repair decisions, so choose the level of involvement that suits you.

The two plans

Fully Managed

Hand over full control

Lowest landlord involvement
  • You hand over full control of day-to-day management
  • GETLET orders repairs directly without prior approval, within written delegated authority
  • Gas and electrical certification arranged through GETLET's agencies
  • Tenant issues handled end to end
  • Costs deducted from rent via PayProp

How we handle one repair

Example: a boiler fault, step by step

An illustrative scenario showing how the same problem moves through each plan.

Fully Managed

  1. Tenant reports the boiler has stopped working.
  2. GETLET checks the property is on Fully Managed.
  3. A heating contractor is booked directly, within the agreed authority.
  4. Contractor attends and repairs or advises.
  5. The invoice is entered in PayProp and deducted from the next rent.
  6. You see it on your statement with the invoice behind it.

Limited Management

  1. Tenant reports the boiler has stopped working.
  2. GETLET checks the property is on Limited Management.
  3. GETLET contacts you with the issue and an estimate, and asks for permission.
  4. You approve. Work starts only after this step.
  5. Contractor attends and repairs or advises.
  6. The approved invoice is entered in PayProp and deducted from the next rent.

Real emergencies, such as a suspected gas leak, are never held up waiting for approval. See the emergency guidance. Emergency handling for each plan is set out in your management agreement.
