Tenant management

Once a tenant moves in, GETLET is the tenant's contact for rent queries and maintenance. How much of that reaches you depends on your plan: in Fully Managed, tenant issues are handled end to end; in Limited Management, we report the issue, get your approval, then dispatch.

Rent collection

Rent is paid into a PayProp-linked account. When it arrives, recorded invoices and expenses are deducted automatically and the remaining balance is paid to you.

Landlord reporting & expense management

Every piece of work done on your property is entered as an invoice, so each deduction can be traced back to the work behind it. That creates an audit trail you can read and check.

Gas & electrical certification coordination

GETLET arranges gas and electrical certificates through specialist partner agencies. GETLET does not carry out certified work itself.

Important: arranging a certificate does not remove the landlord's legal duty. You remain responsible for making sure your property meets the current requirements.
