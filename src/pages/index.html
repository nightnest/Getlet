Maintenance & service standard

Repairs handled by people we have already vetted

When a tenant reports a problem, GETLET checks which management plan applies, sends a contractor from its pre-vetted network and records the invoice, so the cost is traceable from report to rent statement.

GETLET's stated service standard is 24 hours. It is a standard we are working to, not an independently verified guarantee, and access, parts and approvals can affect how long a repair takes. We explain exactly what it means on the repairs page.
